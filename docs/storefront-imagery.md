# Storefront imagery

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool, then exported as quality 88 JPEGs for the website. These are representative category images; they do not describe actual product packaging. Product names, sizes, prices and availability come from the store inventory.

## Final prompts

### Homepage hero

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: homepage hero photograph for Neerra, a fresh milk and drinking water online store. Create a premium editorial food photograph, landscape 3:2. On a warm ivory kitchen counter: a clear glass milk bottle with plain white cap, a full glass of milk, a small ceramic bowl of yogurt, a folded natural linen cloth and a few green leaves. Sunlight from a window, soft realistic shadows, warm morning atmosphere, restrained forest green and creamy white palette. Composition centered on the dairy still life with room around edges for responsive cropping. Real materials, clean sophisticated grocery brand aesthetic. No text, no logos, no lettering, no people, no collage, no interface.

### Milk

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: representative milk category photograph for a grocery product card. Square premium studio product photography. One elegant clear glass bottle filled with fresh white milk, a plain silver or white cap, beside one small glass of milk. Full bottle and glass completely visible with generous breathing room and soft contact shadow. Seamless very light warm ivory background, soft window light, realistic reflections, restrained tasteful grocery catalog aesthetic. No text, no brand, no logos, no decorations, no hands, no watermark.

### Drinking water

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: representative drinking water category photograph for a grocery product card. Square premium studio product photography. One clear reusable water bottle filled with water, a plain pale blue cap, beside one short clear drinking glass of water. Full bottle and glass completely visible with generous breathing room and soft contact shadow. Seamless very pale cool blue background, soft studio window light, realistic translucent materials, restrained tasteful grocery catalog aesthetic. No text, no brand, no logos, no decorations, no hands, no watermark.
